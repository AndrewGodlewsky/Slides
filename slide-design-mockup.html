01 · Design tokens

Canvas: #0d1117 on every slide. Act accent system: each act owns ONE accent color used for its kicker, bullet markers, callout borders, image glow, and ribbon — never mix accents within a slide (the only exceptions: the title, takeaways, and closer slides, which use the full 5-color set deliberately). Semantic colors: amber = caveat/verify, red = refuted/anti-pattern, green doubles as "good/✓" in comparisons regardless of act.

13 · Gemini image system

The one prefix, every prompt:
"Dark futuristic 3D isometric illustration, near-black background (#0d1117), subtle volumetric fog, glossy dark surfaces, clean minimal composition with generous empty space, glowing neon accent lighting in {ACT_COLOR}, cinematic rim light, high detail, professional tech-conference aesthetic, absolutely no text, no words, no letters, no numbers, no logos."
{ACT_COLOR}: emerald green · electric blue · violet purple · hot pink · cyan. Generate in per-act batches for consistency. 2–3 candidates per prompt; pick the emptiest composition. Full-bleed slides 16:9 · side-zone slides 1:1.

ACT 1 · sample — slide 3 (16:9)

PREFIX + A swarm of twenty small chaotic rockets veering in scattered directions across a dark starfield, most tumbling off course with sputtering trails, a distant glowing moon untouched, one sleek confident rocket on a clean precise green-lit trajectory.

ACT 2 · sample — slide 9 (1:1)

PREFIX + A pristine glass machine box on a dark pedestal, a ribbon of luminous blue glyph-like shapes flowing in one side and a different ribbon flowing out the other, the interior showing only elegant clockwork probability gears, no storage, no memory banks.

ACT 3 · sample — slide 17 (16:9)

PREFIX + A dark exchange counter where chunky old arcade tokens on the left are being melted by violet light into a fine stream of thousands of micro-coins flowing to the right, a holographic balance scale above tipping toward the fine stream.

ACT 4 · sample — slide 43 (1:1)

PREFIX + A massive torrent of raw grey data plunging through an elegant small pink mechanical sieve mid-air, only a thin refined luminous stream continuing down into a sleek waiting neural core, the discarded bulk dispersing as harmless dark mist.

ACT 5 · sample — slide 49 (1:1)

PREFIX + A beautiful circular cyan progress ring floating as a holographic instrument above a dark IDE-like workspace, the ring two-thirds filled with the leading edge glowing amber, small orbiting satellite chips feeding the ring.

RESCUE SUFFIXES

too busy → ", minimalist, single focal subject" · too dim → ", brighter accent glow, higher contrast" · fights headline → ", darker, more negative space in upper left"
