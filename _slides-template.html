Model Quirks
  • Sonnet 4.6 ≈ 98% of Opus quality at ~40% less
  • Escalate only when Sonnet stalls — don't default to Opus 4.7
  • GPT-5.3-Codex is Copilot's agentic default — 25% faster, cheaper input than GPT-5.4
  • Long-context price traps — GPT-5.4 / 5.5 double input cost past 272K; Gemini 3.1 Pro jumps past 200K
  • Tier your routing — budget → workhorse → flagship cuts total spend 60–70%
